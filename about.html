Who I am

I'm Alexandre Rocha, a cybersecurity engineer based in Dublin, Ireland, with 14+ years in technology and 7+ years focused on security. I work on both sides of the fence, offensive and defensive, which is to say I like breaking things to understand how to make them safer.

Day to day I lead security across enterprise and multi-tenant cloud environments: running penetration tests and vulnerability management, building detection and response in the SOC (SIEM, EDR and XDR), and tying it together under governance frameworks like ISO 27001, NIST CSF and MITRE ATT&CK.

Offensive Security

Pentesting and purple teaming across web, network and infrastructure.

Detection & Response

SOC operations, threat hunting and EDR/XDR/MDR incident response.

Cloud Security

Securing enterprise and multi-tenant cloud on AWS, Azure and GCP.

Governance & Risk

ISO 27001, NIST CSF and MITRE ATT&CK; vulnerability and risk management.

Outside of that, I build, and everything here is free to use: a live AI news portal (Cyber Pulse), a catalogue of 366 security tools (Arsenal), 50 certification mind maps (Knowledge Base), and hands-on tools like the Career Compass and the Incident Cost Estimator. I write and share what I learn so that people starting out in tech and security have a clearer path.

On the certification side I hold credentials across offensive security, blue team and cloud, including CEH, CrowdStrike CCFA, LLM security (CLLMSP), Fortinet, and the AWS Solutions Architect, Security, AI and Cloud Practitioner tracks.
